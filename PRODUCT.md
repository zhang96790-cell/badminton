# Skyline Smash: product

## What it is
A browser badminton game (singles) at badmintongames.site. Real shuttle aerodynamics, a broadcast-style
court, CPU opponents at five levels and four personalities, local 2-player, and full officiating: umpire
voice calls, line judges, service judge, Hawk-Eye challenges, a 25-second shot clock, replays and
end-of-match stats. Interactive tutorial and practice drills.

## Who plays
The maker plays badminton competitively; friends range from new to experienced. They play on laptops
(keyboard plus mouse or trackpad), usually indoors, often sharing the link with each other.

## What the start screen must do
Get someone into a match fast. Play, Tutorial and Practice up front; difficulty, CPU style, controls,
scoring format, assists and voice live in a settings panel. New players are nudged to the tutorial.
"What's new" opens the changelog pop-up.

## Brand commitments
- Name: **Skyline Smash** (renamed from Badminton Games in 2.0.1). The domain badmintongames.site still redirects here.
- Feel: a pro tournament (a real event's court, officials and broadcast), serious and sporty, not arcade.
- Keep the game's own facts honest: no invented sponsors, players or tournaments presented as real.
