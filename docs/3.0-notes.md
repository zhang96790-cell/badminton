## Phase 0: preflight (what 2.7 left us)

All present, nothing rebuilt:

| Piece | Where |

Also recorded:

- **CPU levels:** `beginner | easy | medium | hard | expert` (`AI` in `src/config.ts`): reaction, speed, strength,
  aim noise, timing error and shot preferences per level. A CPU opponent is an `AIController(game, player, level, style)`;
  styles are `STYLES` (allround, attacker, defender, net, deceptive, retriever, wall).
- **Smash / reach / swing constants:** `SHOTS` (smash speed by contact height, aim model, back court), `PLAYER`
  (reach 1.15 m, +0.1 for people), `SWING` (idealDelay 0.14, perfect ±0.08, hitRecovery 0.45).
- **Stats JSON writer:** `src/career.ts` `careerDelta` → `add_career` RPC (keys `^[a-z_]{1,32}$`; `max_` keeps best,
  `now_` replaces, others add). Note the 32-character limit: `char_wins_<slug>` must stay within it.
- **Settings profiles:** `badminton.profiles` in localStorage (`src/main.ts`), synced to the account settings.

## Owner's decisions for 3.0 (asked before building, 14 Nov 2026)

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Ranked stat totals | Use the sheet's totals as they are (Common 100, Rare 104, Super Rare 108, Epic 113; up to 140 later). `rankedBudgetMode` defaults to `"sheet"`; `"cap10"` is one config line away. |
| Career strength | Both: career-only stronger stat effects (ranked untouched) **and** rarer opponents get a harder CPU level; tuned with simulations to the spec's targets. |
| Daily challenge reward | Rating points, as in the spec (5–15, capped 75 a season, never into World Class), with the token checks. |
| Career starter | A free pick of 1 of 3 Common players from your home country (nearby countries if fewer than 3); it's added to your account. |
| Career difficulty | Chosen when starting a career: Relaxed (as spec), Normal (+1 CPU level), Hard (+2, capped at Expert). Can be raised but not lowered mid-career. |
| Shipping | One 3.0 push after every phase is built and checked, once the SQL is run and the worker deployed. |

## What shipped, by phase

- **A. Roster.** `npm run roster:import` reads the first sheet of the owner's .xlsx (exceljs, with a small built-in
  reader as a fallback because the owner's file uses absolute paths inside the package, which exceljs can't open) or a
  .csv. 435 players import in about 0.25 s. Ids are slugs of names (max 20 letters, fits `char_losses_<id>` in 32);
  the sheet id is kept as `sheet_id`. Two stat sets: `base_stats` (career) and `stats` (ranked; "sheet" mode by
  default, "cap10" one line away in `src/roster/roster-config.json`). Errors fail; warnings go to
  `docs/roster-warnings.md` (25 style/stat clashes, 12 empty ability slots, 65 players with a stat at 10 or 35). The
  roster hash in the online handshake covers everything in roster.json (base, ranked, abilities). Players tab: search,
  filters (rarity, style, country, hand, owned), sorts (name, rarity, total, each stat), a grid that only draws what's
  on screen (about 3 ms to filter and draw), side-by-side compare. Only players released by the game version show
  (3.0: 226 Common + 113 Rare + the Club Player).
- **B. Saves.** `career_saves` with a revision number; `career_get` / `career_put(expected_rev, …)`. A stale save
  returns the newer copy and the player picks one. A local copy covers outages (sent when back). A connection error
  never looks like "no career". Auto-saves after every match and every calendar action.
- **C. Career strength.** `getEffectiveStats(c, modifiers, 'career')`: base stats × (1 + (career_strength − 100)/100 ×
  k), per-point effects twice the ranked ones, limits ±18% (smash −5% to +10%). Rarer opponents get a CPU level from
  their rarity's range; difficulty adds 0/1/2 levels.
- **D. Tournaments.** `data/tournaments.json` (5 Super 300, 6 Super 100, 5 disabled TBD rows). Blank weeks are spaced
  evenly by order (weeks 2, 6, 10 … 42). 16-player draws, rarity-weighted fields, at least 2 host-country players, at
  most 3 per country, at most a third of one style, top 4 seeded into opposite halves, wildcard chance 0.
- **E. World ranking.** Best 10 results in the last 52 weeks, every released player ranked, NPC results from a seeded
  simulator (never stored). The world also plays unseen "world events" (14 Super 100, 8 Super 300 and 6 Super 500 a
  year, NPCs only) so the ranking isn't decided by the 11 events you can enter.
- **F. Money, travel, energy.** All in `src/careermode/config.ts` (the spec's `config/career.ts`; a `src/config/`
  folder would clash with `src/config.ts`). Regions in `data/country_regions.json`.
- **G. Recruiting.** Server-made offers (`career_offers`), one reroll, 3 offers a day, only offered players granted.
- **H. Signatures.** `data/signatures.json` (30 abilities), engine in `src/careermode/signatures.ts`, badge on screen
  when one fires, shown on cards. Career and daily only (`signaturesInRanked = false`).
- **I. Daily challenge.** Chosen by the server from the date (so nobody can pick an easier one), token, 90 s minimum,
  3-hour expiry, score checks, one claim a day, 75 a season, ceiling 1699.
- **J. Screens.** Career panel (This week, Events, Ranking, Squad, Money, Daily challenge, Settings), tournament
  detail, draw and results, recruiting, season recap, conflict choice, new-career setup. Court 6 on the start screen.

## Simulations

**1. Strength gaps** (career stats, hard CPU both sides, best of 3 to 21, random real players of each rarity, sides
swapped; 84–120 matches each, so about ±9%):

| Settings | Rare vs Common |
|---|---|
| As first built (k 0.15, effects as ranked ×1.6) | 53.6% |
| k 0.5 | 56.0% |
| k 0.5, effects ×2 | 67.9% |
| k 1.0, effects ×2 | 75.0% |

Chosen: k 0.4, effects ×2 (120 matches each):

| Matchup | Target | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Rare vs Common (adjacent) | 58–68% | 68.3% |
| Super Rare vs Common (two apart) | 70–82% | 80.0% |
| Epic vs Common (three apart) | ≤ 90% | 80.8% |
| Super Rare vs Rare (adjacent) | 58–68% | 53.3% (miss) |

The miss: Super Rares and Epics hit the ±18% limits, so above Rare more rarity barely helps. Only Common and Rare are out
in 3.0, so the 3.0 gap is on target. **For 3.1:** widen the career limits or reshape the curve before Super Rares ship.
The strongest smash stayed at +10% (about 405 km/h against 366 for a neutral player).

**2. Energy** (the same Common player on both sides, one starting the match at the given energy; best of 3 to 21,
120 matches each):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 60 | 12% (spec) | 50.8% (±9.1%): no change | 50.1% | 396 / 398 |
| 0 | 12% (spec) | 29.2% (±8.3%): a 21-point drop, too much | 47.3% | 399 / 393 |

net kills). Falcon Counter rarely fires against the CPU (it needs a smash returned with a drive or push).

## Defaults chosen

- Starter pick: 3 Commons from your home country, topped up from your region (then anywhere) if fewer than 3.
- Week actions: "Next week" gives +5 energy for free; "Rest week" +15 for 60. Food and physio don't use the week.
- Entering needs travel plus one hotel night. Withdrawing before your first match earns nothing.
- Faster early rounds (off by default): rounds before the quarter-finals are a single game to 15.
- Stamina (career stats) changes energy use by 1.5% per point away from 20.
- Last Stand (energy use) is the only ability that acts outside a match.
- Deception abilities reuse the existing disguise effect (late landing marker, wrong-footed step) as a chance per shot.
- Abilities that cut the opponent's smash can't push it below the career smash floor (−5%).
- The daily challenge is one game (to 21, or 11 with that handicap); the 11-point format exists only for it.
- Daily opponent CPU level: Hard for Rares and up, Medium for Commons.
- Ranking movement compares with the week before.
- `max_career_best_rank` stores 1000 − your best rank (stats keep the highest), so the best ranking sorts first.
- Career stats keys: `career_matches`, `career_wins`, `career_titles`, `career_prize`, `max_career_best_rank`.
- Event order is the order in the spec's list, interleaved by tier; the owner can set real weeks in the data file.
- The 2.7 rank-up picks now only offer released players (they would have offered unreleased Super Rares and Epics).
