Assists & controls

Landing marker

A ring on the floor where the shuttle will come down. In ranked matches it only shows where your own shots will land.

Timing light

Yellow means wait, green means swing, red means you’re late.

Swing assist

Gets lifts, drops and clears back if you don’t swing. Smashes still need you. Only against Beginner and Easy, and every assisted shot is tagged ASSIST.

Performance mode

For slow school laptops: a lighter crowd, lower resolution, fewer effects and 30 frames a second. Can also make your ping look better, since a busy laptop answers the server late.

Auto-switch (doubles)

You take over whichever of your pair is better placed for the next shot.

Umpire voice

Spoken score calls and line calls.

Online chat

Quick-chat lines from the other player (keys 1–6 send yours).

Your keys

Click an action, then press its new key (Esc cancels). Taking a key another action uses swaps them. Two players on one keyboard keep the standard keys.
